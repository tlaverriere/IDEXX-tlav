How streaks work

Your daily streak measures perfection. Your week streak measures consistency. They're kept separate on purpose — losing one doesn't touch the other.

Daily streak

  • Counts consecutive days with a completed lesson.
  • One missed day ends it — unless you have a freeze.
  • Your best ever stays on your profile for good.
  • Protection can never be bought. Only earned.

Freezes

  • A freeze covers one missed day and keeps your daily streak alive.
  • Applied automatically. We'll tell you when one gets used.
  • You earn one for every 4 weeks with no missed days at all.
  • You start with one, and get another at the start of week 5 and week 9. After that they're earned only.
  • You can hold up to 7 — enough to cover a week away.
  • A day covered by a freeze still counts as a missed day for your week streak.
  • A frozen day doesn't count toward earning your next freeze.

Week streak

  • Counts consecutive weeks where you hit your weekly goal.
  • The goal: miss no more than one day, plus any spare days you've saved.
  • Your weeks run from the day you joined — not Monday to Sunday.

Spare days

  • You start with 3.
  • Any week you don't use your allowance, it banks — up to 3.
  • Spent automatically when you need them.
  • With all 3 saved you can miss 4 days in one week and still keep your week streak.

Last-gasp save

  • Miss your weekly goal and you can commit to 7 days in a row to save your week streak.
  • Entirely your choice. If you don't finish the run, it costs you nothing.
  • Four per year.

Neither streak protects against not showing up at all. That part's still on you — but a missed day here and there was never meant to undo your work.
